The app

Tempo is a calendar and task app for iPhone and iPad. The core calendar is free to use. Tempo reads from and writes to the calendars already set up on your device, so your events stay where they are (iCloud, Google, etc.). Once you allow access to Reminders, your tasks are reminders in a "Tempo" list in Apple's Reminders app.

Tempo Plus subscriptions

Tempo Plus (shown in the app as Tempo+) is an optional auto-renewable subscription. It unlocks these extra features:

  • The auto-scheduler. Tempo suggests times on your calendar to work on your tasks. Without Tempo Plus, you can accept two of its sessions to try it.
  • Project planning. Tempo spreads a project's sessions across your free time. Anyone can turn on project mode and see its forecast. Planning the sessions needs Tempo Plus.
  • The procrastination calculator. A task's latest possible start, and its Reality check of how much working time is left before its deadline.
  • Travel. On iPhone and iPad: travel time to events that have a location, shown on your calendar; "time to leave" alerts; and leave-by times on the "Where am I going?" widget.
  • The leave-by time on the Lock Screen card that appears about an hour before an event. The card itself is free.
  • Trips. Extra time zones when you travel, the hotel-stay template, and "Add from flight" in the Day view's + menu.
  • AI features. Ask Tempo, the in-app assistant, and Quick Add's AI help.
  • Reviews. Meeting prep and the weekly review.
  • Smart suggestions. When an event clashes with another, Tempo points it out and suggests a new time. It also suggests a length for a new event and an energy level for a new task, based on the words in the title.
