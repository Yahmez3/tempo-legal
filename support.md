# Tempo Support

Tempo is built by an independent developer in Australia. The fastest way to get help is email.

**Contact:** [tempo.studios@proton.me](mailto:tempo.studios@proton.me)

You can usually expect a reply within 1–2 business days.

## Common questions

### How do I cancel my Tempo+ subscription?

Apple handles subscriptions, not the app itself. Open the Settings app → tap your name at the top → **Subscriptions** → tap Tempo's subscription → **Cancel Subscription**. You can also reach this from inside Tempo: tap the gear to open **Settings**, then tap **Manage Subscription** in the Tempo+ section.

Cancelling lets your subscription, or your free trial, run to the end of the current period, then stops. Your calendar, tasks, notes and everything you've created stay intact — Tempo+ features just turn off.

### How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with. Then in Tempo, open **Settings** and tap **Restore Purchases** in the Tempo+ section.

### The app crashed / something looks broken

If **Share crash reports** is on (under Privacy in Tempo's Settings), Tempo sends me an anonymous report automatically. It carries nothing that identifies you, so I can't match it to your email. Please also email me at [tempo.studios@proton.me](mailto:tempo.studios@proton.me) with:

- Your iPhone or iPad model and its iOS or iPadOS version (Settings → General → About)
- Tempo's version (the **Version** row at the bottom of Tempo's Settings)
- What you were doing when it happened
- A screenshot if you can grab one

### A request, an idea, something you wish Tempo did

I read everything. [tempo.studios@proton.me](mailto:tempo.studios@proton.me).

## Privacy

The privacy policy is at [privacy.html](privacy.html). The short version: Tempo has no accounts. Your events stay in your calendars. Once you give Tempo access to Reminders, your tasks are reminders in Apple's Reminders app. Until then, Tempo keeps them in its own storage on your device. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell data. Tempo's server keeps only two things: a count of AI requests for each install, kept under a random ID, and anonymous crash reports. You can turn crash reports off under Privacy in Tempo's Settings. Ask Tempo and Quick Add's AI help are part of Tempo+. They send some of your information to Anthropic through our server, but only after you tap **Allow**. Anthropic's terms don't allow it to train its models on that information.
